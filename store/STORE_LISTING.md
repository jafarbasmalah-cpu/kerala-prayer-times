## Short description (max 80)
Accurate namaz times for Kerala with adhan, Qibla, adhkar and Ramadan timetable

## Full description (English)
Basmalah gives you accurate daily namaz times for all 14 districts of Kerala, calculated the Kerala way and matched to the published Kerala timetable.

★ ACCURATE TIMES
• Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for every district headquarters
• Kerala standard calculation (Fajr & Isha 18°, Shafi'i Asr, IST), or use your exact GPS location
• Adjust any prayer by a minute or two to match your local masjid

★ ADHAN
• Adhan at every prayer, or a simple notification – your choice for each prayer
• Choose from several adhan sounds, with a separate Fajr adhan
• Reminder before each prayer
• Adhan reliability check that guides you through your phone's battery settings

★ HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• Next prayer with live countdown and today's times on your home screen

★ QIBLA
• Live compass that starts by itself, with bearing and distance to Makkah

★ ADHKAR, DUAS & TASBIH
• After-prayer adhkar and duas for each prayer: Arabic, transliteration, meaning (English & Malayalam) and source
• Tasbih counter with targets of 33, 99 or 100

★ RAMADAN & HIJRI CALENDAR
• Ramadan mode with Suhoor and Iftar times and countdown
• Full Ramadan timetable to share with family
• Hijri calendar with Islamic days (Nabidinam, Badr day, Eid and more)
• Jumu'ah reminder every Friday

★ MORE
• Monthly timetable – share it as an image or print it
• English and Malayalam
• Light and dark theme
• Works offline. No ads. No account. No data collected.
