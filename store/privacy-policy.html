Basmalah – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Basmalah ("the app") shows prayer times, adhan alerts, the Qibla direction, adhkar and a Hijri calendar. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Location

If you choose "Use my exact location (GPS)", the app reads your location on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can choose a district instead and never share your location.

Notifications, alarms and motion sensors

Notification and alarm permissions are used only to play the adhan and reminders at prayer times. The compass (motion sensors) is used only on the Qibla screen. None of this information leaves your phone.

Settings

Your district, language and adhan choices are stored on your phone so the app remembers them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
