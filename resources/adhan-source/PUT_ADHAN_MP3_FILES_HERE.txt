Put your adhan recordings in this folder, named exactly like this (MP3 recommended):

  makkah.mp3         Makkah – Masjid al-Haram
  madinah.mp3        Madinah – Masjid an-Nabawi
  alaqsa.mp3         Al-Aqsa – Jerusalem
  mishary.mp3        Mishary Rashid Alafasy
  egypt.mp3          Egypt – Classic
  kerala.mp3         Kerala masjid
  fajr_makkah.mp3    Fajr adhan – Makkah   (with "As-salatu khairum minan-nawm")
  fajr_madinah.mp3   Fajr adhan – Madinah

Then run:  npm run setup:native   and   npx cap sync

Any sound you don't add shows "Not installed" in the app and falls back to the built-in chime.
Only use recordings you have permission to use (for example, recorded at your own masjid,
or ones whose licence allows use in an app).
