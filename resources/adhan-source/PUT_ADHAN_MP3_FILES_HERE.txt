The build downloads 5 freely licensed adhans automatically (Makkah, Madinah, Aaqib Azeez, Short adhan, Fajr – Besim Azemi).

To add your own adhan, put an MP3 here that you have permission to use, for example:
  mishary.mp3      -> appears in the app as "Mishary"
  fajr_kerala.mp3  -> appears as a Fajr adhan (name starts with "fajr")
  makkah.mp3       -> replaces the built-in Makkah recording
Then upload to GitHub; the next build includes it.
